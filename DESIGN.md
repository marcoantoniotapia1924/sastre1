## Brand & Style
The brand embodies quiet luxury (*lujo sobrio*), bespoke Peruvian craftsmanship, and modern sartorial precision. Rooted in Lima's heritage of fine tailoring and high-end Made to Measure culture, the interface channels the exclusivity of a private atelier: discreet, confident, meticulously proportioned, and tactile.

The design philosophy bridges classical Savile Row discipline with contemporary Latin American luxury. It avoids ostentatious ornamentation in favor of architectural balance, generous negative space, crisp rectilinear contours, and micro-tailoring cues (haute couture stitching patterns, fine thread-weight divider rules, and restrained metallic leaf accents). The user journey evokes an exclusive consultation: serene, authoritative, and friction-free.

## Colors
The palette evokes the textures of superfine alpaca wool, worsted navy suiting cloth, and horn buttons:

- **Primary (`#0B1426` — Deep Midnight Navy):** The structural backbone. Conveys absolute authority, timeless tailoring tradition, and masculine depth. Used for dominant typographic statements, high-emphasis action triggers, framing containers, and dark-panel interludes.
- **Secondary (`#B8975A` — Polished Muted Gold):** Reserved for prestige insignias, monogram accents, subtle filigree dividers, active indicators, and secondary high-touch triggers. Applied with restraint to prevent ostentation.
- **Tertiary (`#2B2B2B` — Charcoal Black):** The functional ink for editorial body text, subheadings, fine border definitions, and tactile metadata.
- **Neutral (`#F7F4EE` — Warm Ivory / Parchment):** The foundation canvas. Replaces sterile digital white with the warmth of unbleached tailor’s chalk paper and raw shirting linen.

### Supporting Tone Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F7F4EE`
- **Surface Elevation / Atelier Cards:** `#FFFFFF` (pure white pop for fabric swatches and measurement modules)
- **Subtle Seam Border:** `rgba(11, 20, 38, 0.08)`
- **Sartorial Gold Glow / Focus Ring:** `rgba(184, 151, 90, 0.35)`

## Typography
Typographic rhythm mirrors an editorial atelier catalogue. 

- **Playfair Display** commands titles and display tiers with classical gravitas, high-contrast strokes, and graceful serifs reminiscent of vintage bespoke trade plaques.
- **Inter** provides high legibility for technical garments, cloth weight specifications, and consultation booking flows. Its neutral, engineered cadence keeps the product grounded in contemporary utility without clashing with the serif headlines.
- **Tracking & Proportion:** Section kickers, garment tags, and tailoring step indicators employ `label-caps` set strictly in uppercase with open letter spacing (`0.14em`) to mirror luxury house catalog standards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column architectural grid on desktop (`1200px` to `1440px` max-width container) and a single-column structured stack on mobile. Negative space is treated as an active luxury material: expansive canvas areas convey patience, craftsmanship, and bespoke unhurriedness.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, generous 56px margins. Margins expand to auto-centered frames on ultra-wide screens.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px margins.
- **Sartorial Rhythms:** Vertical spacing alternates between tight module groupings (`space-sm` to `space-md` for metadata and specs) and deliberate section gaps (`space-xl` and above) to let each fabric collection and tailoring step breathe.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is flat, crisp, and tactile rather than synthetic. The system rejects deep blurry drop shadows in favor of low-contrast hairline borders, crisp silhouettes, and micro-diffused ambient lifts:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Ivory `#F7F4EE` canvas layered directly above structural panels.
- **Level 1 (Fabric & Service Cards):** Solid `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid rgba(11, 20, 38, 0.08)`) and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow (`0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 20, 38, 0.04)`).
- **Level 2 (Overlays, VIP Drawers, Date Pickers):** Elevated via `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(11, 20, 38, 0.10)` paired with an inner top border tinted in gold (`1px solid rgba(184, 151, 90, 0.3)`).
- **Stitch Seam Motifs:** Subtle dashed hairline separators (`border-top: 1px dashed rgba(11, 20, 38, 0.15)`) evoke hand-basting thread during fittings.

## Shapes
Geometry is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Sharp corners emphasize the cutting shears of the master tailor (*maestro sastre*), crisp lapels, and architectural suit shoulders. 

- Form inputs, buttons, fabric swatches, imagery containers, and modals all carry `0px` border radius (`rounded-none`).
- Occasional decorative badges or monograms may feature a subtle chamfered or 1px hairline border, maintaining strict angular purity throughout.

## Components

### Primary Consultation & WhatsApp Actions
- **Bespoke Booking Button:** Primary action rendered in Deep Midnight Navy (`#0B1426`) with crisp white or ivory typography, `0px` border-radius, `16px 32px` padding, uppercase track-spaced label (`label-caps`), and a deliberate 1px gold hover transition (`#B8975A`).
- **Direct WhatsApp Concierge Action:** Rich dark emerald-tinted midnight or Navy base paired with a dedicated gold pip or monogram indicator, direct messaging label *"Coordinar Cita vía WhatsApp"*, and immediate tactile state change.

### Secondary & Atelier Actions
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined hairline button with `1px solid #0B1426` or `1px solid #B8975A`, transparent background, and inverted fill on cursor interaction.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Underlined with a crisp, 1px baseline stroke spaced 4px beneath the text, shifting to gold on focus.

### Fabric Swatches & Selection Chips
- Rectilinear cards (`rounded-none`) displaying cloth mill origins (e.g., Loro Piana, Scabal, Peruvian Baby Alpaca), weight in grams, and weave texture. Active selection is denoted by a solid 1.5px `#0B1426` outer border and a delicate inner `#B8975A` corner accent.

### Inputs & Appointment Date Pickers
- **Text Inputs:** Sharp-edged rectangular containers with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid rgba(11, 20, 38, 0.15)` border, and floating label in Charcoal (`#2B2B2B`). On active focus, the border shifts to `#0B1426` with a delicate golden outline tint.
- **Fittings Calendar:** Editorial grid with sharp square cells, subtle ivory borders, and gold-filled indicators for reserved bespoke fitting slots.

### Cards & Sartorial Case Studies
- **Lookbook & Service Card:** High-aspect-ratio photography bordered by hairline parchment seams. Hover reveals a slow, elegant scale transition (0.3s ease-out) with zero edge softening, accompanied by Playfair Display headlines and metadata in uppercase Inter tracking.
